BRUNO M GIUFFRÈ — DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Upload index.html, style.css, Bruno-Giuffre.vcf and the assets folder to your GitHub repository root. Keep the folder structure.
3. In repository Settings > Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, main, / (root), then Save.
4. Use your GitHub Pages URL on the NFC tag.

Open index.html for a local preview. The page uses local assets and system fonts; no paid services or dependencies.

CONTACT BUTTONS
Call mobile, Email, Directions and Save contact are linked. The office phone and email are also clickable in the details section. Contact saving may show a download or contact preview depending on the device.

PORTRAIT
No portrait was included in the supplied files. To add his actual photo later, save it as assets/portrait.jpg. The page will display it automatically. It does not use a stock or invented doctor portrait.

ASSETS
The supplied raster crests are included unchanged. These are PNG files, not vector SVGs. Hospital background pattern is part of the supplied image. Lettering alongside both crests is live HTML text, vertically centred.
Decorative globe contours suggest imaging technology; they are not diagnostic scan images.

Information and legacy institutional wording are transcribed from the original card. Original-card.jpeg is included for reference. Check the contact details before using the card publicly.

EDITING
Edit index.html to change displayed details and button links. Edit Bruno-Giuffre.vcf as well when contact details change. Edit style.css for colours, spacing and typography.
