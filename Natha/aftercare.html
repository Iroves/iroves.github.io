AFTER YOUR TATTOO

Give it time.
Give it care.

  1. Follow your dressing instructions

    Cling wrap and second-skin dressings have different removal instructions. Follow the timing given for your dressing; ask Nathaly if you’re unsure.

  2. Clean gently

    Wash your hands first. Once the dressing is removed as instructed, gently wash with mild soap and running water, then pat dry with a clean towel.

  3. Protect the healing skin

    Avoid swimming, soaking, direct sun and clothing that rubs the area. Don’t scratch or pick at peeling skin or scabs.

  4. Use the recommended aftercare

    Use only the care product and amount recommended by Nathaly or a health professional.

A QUESTION ABOUT YOUR TATTOO?

Let’s check in.

For a question about the instructions you were given, contact Nathaly. If the area becomes very red, swollen, painful, has ongoing bleeding or discharge, or you’re worried about infection, seek medical advice promptly.
