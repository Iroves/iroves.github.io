NATHA.TAT — OWNER GUIDE
21 October 2026

ONE STARTING POINT
Clients can explore tattoos, request an appointment, buy art, read aftercare
and ask about party scenography or other creative projects from one website.
The website and this guide use English throughout.

1. TATTOO BOOKINGS
Book explains the request process: idea and references, placement and size,
two or three available dates, your proposal and the deposit link.
The actual request is submitted through your current Venue Ink page. Your
private calendar, proposals and booking deposits remain there. No bookings
were created or migrated during development.

2. KEEPING PRODUCTS UP TO DATE
In Shopify Admin, open Products, choose the item, edit its photos, name,
price, description, options and availability, and save. Publish new products
to the Online Store channel. Keep new catalogue text in English.
Shop refreshes the public catalogue on opening and when Refresh is pressed.
If the request fails, it shows a labelled saved copy. Checkout confirms the
current price, stock and delivery options. These updates do not change the
poster or the tattoo and art portfolio photographs.
Known Portuguese wording in the existing catalogue is displayed in English
on this site. This is a small local wording map, not a general translation
service for future product descriptions. New or edited wording should be
written in English in Shopify. Product IDs and checkout links are preserved.

3. PAYMENT LINKS FOR TATTOOS AND OTHER SERVICES
Clients can request the correct payment link from Book > Payments.
After agreeing on the work and price, create a Shopify draft order and send
an invoice with its checkout link, using the features available in your account.
Check which payment methods the specific order offers before promising Afterpay.
The website does not issue invoices or payment links automatically and does
not confirm that a payment was received. Booking deposits remain linked to
the proposal in Venue Ink. Use the correct booking or service reference.
Official guide:
https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/create-orders

4. SCENOGRAPHY AND CREATIVE PROJECTS
Creative projects introduces party scenography, commissions and collaborations.
Clients can share an idea, date, location, dimensions and budget range through
WhatsApp, SMS or the contact form. Agree on scope, price and payment directly.
The current image is labelled as a painting from your portfolio. Photographs
of real completed events can be added later with accurate project descriptions.

5. PREPARATION AND AFTERCARE
Care brings session preparation and general tattoo-care guidance together,
with source links. The individual instructions you provide for each tattoo
and dressing take priority. Care is linked from the home page and navigation.

6. CONTACT WITHOUT INSTAGRAM OR WHATSAPP
Clients can call, send an SMS or use the enquiry form. The form uses FormSubmit
independently of Shopify and still requires inbox activation. Submit a clearly
labelled setup enquiry on the final domain, confirm the activation email and
verify delivery before relying on this route. No test or activation message
was sent during development. Calls, SMS and WhatsApp remain available.

7. ONE PUBLIC ADDRESS; EXISTING SERVICES BEHIND IT
Share this website's final public URL on the NFC tag and social profiles.
This package does not cancel domains or subscriptions, or change payout times.
A domain and a platform subscription are different things; check your invoices
before deciding what to remove. Shopify and Venue Ink still run the store
and booking processes. Do not cancel the old site before publishing this one,
configuring the domain and verifying old links, catalogue, contact, booking
and checkout. Orders and appointments were not moved to a new private dashboard.

8. PUBLISH THE COMPLETE ZIP
Extract everything. Keep index.html and assets/ together at the hosting root.
Upload all files, including the eight HTML pages and unified.css. Configure
the final address and write that public URL to the NFC tag. The private review
preview is not the public customer address. Keep a backup before replacing files.
