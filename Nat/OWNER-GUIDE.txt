THE NEW FLOW
Clients tap the NFC card, view your site and send a tattoo request directly.
You review their idea and dates, agree on the work, send deposit instructions,
verify the payment and confirm the appointment yourself.
The client pages no longer use an external booking platform.
This is a manual approval flow, not an automatic calendar or booking database.
Existing appointments and deposits remain wherever you originally arranged them.

1. RECEIVING REQUESTS AND REFERENCE PICTURES
Book collects the client’s details, idea, placement, size, preferred dates and
payment preference. Both Book and the general enquiry form accept optional
photos/PDFs: up to three files, 9 MB combined. Files are sent through FormSubmit
to your inbox; they are not saved in the public website folder.
The WhatsApp button prepares the request as text. Clients attach their pictures
in WhatsApp themselves. Email is the route that includes selected form files.

2. ACTIVATE EMAIL BEFORE RELYING ON IT
The receiving email is nathalymelomanso@gmail.com. Submit a labelled setup test
on your final hosted domain, confirm FormSubmit’s activation email and verify
that a request and a harmless attachment arrive correctly. No activation email
or test message was sent during development. Calls, SMS and WhatsApp are available
while setup is pending. Once verified, update the activation wording in the HTML
pages and contact.js. Keep the final domain in the setup/delivery check.

3. AGREE ON THE BOOKING BEFORE TAKING A DEPOSIT
Review the idea and reference images. Agree on the session date, an estimated
price range, deposit amount and the applicable cancellation/refund terms directly
with the client. No terms or deposit amount have been invented on your behalf.
Use the request reference or your own agreed reference to identify the payment.
Put the agreed appointment into the calendar you use and check for conflicts.
The website does not check availability, reserve slots or send reminders.

4. PAYID
Confirm your registered PayID and the exact recipient name shown by your bank.
To show them on the site, edit these two fields in booking-settings.js:
payid: the registered receiving identifier
recipientName: the name clients should see in their banking app
These are receiving details only. Never add bank passwords, card details or API
secrets to this file. Both fields are empty until you supply confirmed details.
Until configured, clients are asked to request the details with their proposal.
Clients copy the PayID, enter the agreed amount in their banking app and include
the agreed reference. Check receipt in your own bank account before confirming
the booking. A payment screenshot is not verification. Bank terms still apply;
this site does not promise every transfer is instant or free.

5. SHOPIFY DEPOSIT OR SERVICE PAYMENT
Create a Shopify draft order/invoice for the agreed deposit or service amount
and send its specific secure checkout link to the client. Use a clear description
and the agreed reference so you can identify what the payment covers.
Check the payment options actually offered before promising Afterpay.
Shopify processing fees and settlement terms still apply. No next-day payout
is guaranteed by this site. Shopify invoice creation is manual in this version.
Official guide:
https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/create-orders
The art shop continues to use normal product/variant checkout links.

6. UPDATE YOUR PRODUCTS
In Shopify Admin > Products, update photos, title, price, description, variants
and stock, then save. Publish new products to the Online Store channel.
The site refreshes that public catalogue on opening and on Refresh. A labelled
saved copy is available if the live request fails. Keep new text in English.
This does not update the poster or tattoo/art portfolio photographs.

7. CREATIVE PROJECTS AND CARE
Creative projects promotes party scenography, commissions and collaborations.
The current image is labelled as a portfolio painting. Real project photos can
be added later with accurate descriptions. Care brings preparation and general
tattoo-care information together; your individual dressing instructions prevail.

8. PUBLISH THE FULL ZIP
Keep index.html, the other seven HTML pages, scripts, styles and assets/ together
at the hosting root. Configure the final public domain and write it to the NFC
tag. The private review preview is not a public customer address.
This update does not cancel old services or move historical appointments,
customer records, deposits, subscriptions or domains. Keep a backup before
replacing files and verify the new request, file-delivery and payment process
before retiring the old workflow.
